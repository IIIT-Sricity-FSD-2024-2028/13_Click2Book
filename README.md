13_Click2Book


Your Seat is just a click away

Problem Statement:- The online ticket booking system is designed to simplify and automate the entire process of ticket reservation for users. It enables passengers to search for available trips and check seat availability in real time based on their travel preferences such as source, destination, and date of journey. By providing an online booking facility, the system eliminates the need for users to visit physical ticket counters, thereby reducing long waiting times and manual effort. Real-time processing ensures immediate booking confirmation and accurate seat allocation, which improves overall operational efficiency. The system minimizes human errors that are common in traditional manual booking methods and ensures reliable record management. It also offers a convenient and user-friendly interface that allows users to manage their reservations, view booking details, and perform cancellations when required. Secure handling of user and payment data further increases trust and reliability. Overall, the online ticket booking system enhances customer experience by delivering a fast, accurate, and efficient solution for modern travel ticket reservations.

Identified Actors

The system involves four key actors: Customer, Administrator (Admin), Customer Support, and Service Provider. Each actor interacts with the system according to their roles, responsibilities, and access permissions.

Customer:- Customers are the end-users who use the platform to make and manage reservations. They can register and log in securely to access the system. Customers can search for available services, view schedules, check seat availability, and review pricing details. They are able to make online reservations, select preferred seats, and complete payments through integrated online payment options. After successful booking, customers can view their booking history, download or receive booking confirmations through email or SMS, and manage their reservations by modifying or cancelling them according to the defined policy. Customers can also submit queries or complaints to customer support. Where permitted, they may create reservations on behalf of others, modify bookings, handle group reservations, process assisted payments, generate confirmations, and access real-time availability and booking details.

Administrator (Admin):- Administrators are responsible for managing the overall system and controlling platform operations. They access an administrative dashboard through which they manage users such as customers and support staff. Administrators can add, update, or remove services, manage pricing structures, control schedules and availability, assign roles and permissions, and monitor bookings and financial transactions. They generate system reports and analytics, handle system-level configurations and issues, and approve or manage service providers operating within the platform.

Customer Support:- Customer support staff ensure a smooth service experience by assisting users with their concerns. They log into the system securely to access customer profiles and booking histories. They receive and respond to customer queries, resolve complaints and issues, and track ticket status from open to resolved. They communicate with customers via chat or email, escalate complex issues to the administrator when necessary, and maintain proper records of support logs and feedback for service improvement.

Service Provider:- Service providers are responsible for offering the actual services available for reservation on the platform. They log into the system to add and manage the services they offer. Service providers update availability, manage schedules, and view reservations related to their services. They may confirm or reject bookings if required by business rules, update service status such as available, unavailable, or delayed, monitor revenue and booking statistics, and communicate with the administrator for approvals or operational issues.
